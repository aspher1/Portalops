# Roadmap

## Production foundation

- Verified authentication, organizations, invitations, RBAC, and audited impersonation
- PostgreSQL repositories, transactional state machine, durable queue, leases, cancellation, and idempotent resume
- Isolated Playwright workers, encrypted connector vault, domain-enforced egress, and artifact storage
- Approval inbox with notifications, expiry, delegation, comments, and optimistic concurrency

## Connector readiness

- Versioned payer connector contracts and health checks
- Recorded synthetic fixtures for locator drift, MFA, CAPTCHA, timeouts, and portal maintenance
- Operator-assisted login and explicit escalation states
- Field-level confidence, provenance, and reconciliation exports

## Enterprise operations

- SSO/SAML, SCIM, granular custom roles, retention/legal hold, regional storage, and signed webhooks
- Metrics, tracing, alerting, cost controls, disaster recovery, and connector SLAs
- Independent security and privacy reviews; compliance assertions only after evidence supports them

## Product expansion

- Roster reconciliation, recredentialing packets, directory attestation, delegated approval policies
- Workflow builder with safe templates and simulation
- Integrations for credentialing systems, ticketing, and data warehouses
